Racer is a portrait, retro pseudo-3D roguelike racer. A run is five generated tracks of about a minute, against a checkpoint timer. Every track is proven drivable by a human-like bot before you see it.

After each track the road forks. One side is easier, the other harder, and each side offers an upgrade that stacks. Drift through bends to charge a boost. From the third track, some corners need that boost.

You steer with one thumb: drag sideways to steer, up to drift, down to brake. Buttons are available if you prefer them. A keyboard or a controller works too.

This build is the full edition. It uses its own application id, com.cascadiacollections.racer.foss, so it installs beside a store build and does not replace it.

There are no ads, no in-app purchases, and no network access.

Racer is free and open-source software under the MIT License. It is an early prototype. The name, the balance, and the pictures may still change. The pictures are drawn in code, and the sound is generated by tools/gen_audio.py.
